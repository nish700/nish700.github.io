Numbaloo Privacy Policy

Effective: 15 August 2026 · Contact: optimnetlab@gmail.com

Numbaloo ("the app", "we") is an offline-first mathematics learning app for children aged 5–8. This policy describes exactly what the app does with data — which is very little, by design.

What the app stores

  • Profile details you enter: a child's first name (or nickname) and a chosen avatar emoji, used only to label progress inside the app.
  • Play progress: coins, streaks, completed quests, per-skill practice counters, and settings (language, sound, session limit).
  • Math Mission photos: pictures taken for the weekly photo mission.

All of this is stored on the device, in the app's private storage. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs, and makes no network calls of its own.

What leaves the device — and what never does

  • Play progress and settings are included in Android's standard device backup to your own Google account, so a new phone can restore them. This backup is operated by Google under Google's terms; you can disable device backup in Android Settings at any time.
  • Photos never leave the device. Math Mission photos are stored only in the app's private folder and are explicitly excluded from device backups and phone-to-phone transfers.
  • The progress card (a picture summarising progress) is shared only when a parent taps the share button, through an app the parent chooses (e.g., WhatsApp). The app never sends it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

The optional family plan is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store payment details. Purchases can only be started from Parent Mode, behind an adult gate.

Voice

Narration uses the text-to-speech engine on your device. The app does not operate any speech service of its own.

Children's data

The app is designed for children under Google Play's Families policy and with the data-minimisation and purpose-limitation principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. The only personal data processed — a first name/nickname and optional photos — stays under the family's control on the family's device.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes all app data on the phone. Backup copies in your Google account are managed by Google's backup service and can be removed from your Google account's backup settings; Google also expires app backups automatically after an extended period of inactivity.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the app update that changes the behavior — the policy always describes what the installed version actually does. Questions or grievances: optimnetlab@gmail.com.
