Forkcount — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Forkcount is a local-first eating-out tracker for Android. It is designed to work without an account and to collect as little data as possible. This policy explains what data the app handles, what (if anything) leaves your device, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Everything you log — meals, costs, caps, habits, streaks — stays on your device.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no Forkcount server.
  • We collect anonymous usage events and crash reports to improve the app. Both can be turned off in Settings, and neither includes your logs, costs, or identity.
  • We never sell data, and we never share it for advertising.

Data stored on your device

Your eating-out and cooking logs, meal types and costs, monthly cap, habit check-offs, reminder times, and app settings are stored in a local database on your device. Forkcount has no server and cannot read this data. It is included in your device's standard Android Auto Backup (backed up to your own Google Drive under Google's terms), so your history survives a reinstall or a new phone. You can turn device backup off in Android's system settings. You can also export your data yourself at any time via Settings → Export to CSV; the exported file goes only where you choose to save it.

Data that leaves your device

Three third-party services receive limited technical data. All transfers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

ServicePurposeWhat it receives
PostHog (analytics) Understanding which features are used so we can improve the app Anonymous usage events (e.g. "a log was made", "a cap was set", "the paywall was viewed", app opened/backgrounded) tied to a random install identifier. We never call identify(), so events are not linked to you. No costs, no log contents, no cap amounts, no screen recordings, and no session replay are sent.
Sentry (crash reporting) Finding and fixing crashes and errors Crash and error reports: stack traces, app version, device model, and OS version. No personal information, screenshots, or screen contents.
Google Play Billing / RevenueCat (purchases) Processing optional Forkcount Plus purchases and restoring them Purchase tokens and entitlement status from Google Play, tied to a random app user identifier. Payment itself is handled entirely by Google Play; Forkcount never sees your payment details, name, or email address.

Forkcount contains no advertising SDKs and requests no location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

Your choices

  • Opt out of analytics and crash reporting: turn off Settings → Share anonymous usage data in the app. Nothing is sent afterwards.
  • Notifications: the nightly check-in, habit reminders, and weekly summary are optional and controlled in the app and in Android's notification settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) deletes all locally stored data. To remove the Auto Backup copy as well, delete the app's backup from your Google Drive backups or uninstall with backup disabled.

Data retention

Your logs stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Anonymous analytics events and crash reports are retained by PostHog and Sentry under their standard retention policies and are not linked to your identity. Since analytics are anonymous, we have no way to locate and delete events belonging to a specific person.

Children

Forkcount is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example, if optional bank linking is ever added — this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be noted in the app.
