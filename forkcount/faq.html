Forkcount — FAQ

Common questions about the app. Can't find yours? Email nishant7.pandey@gmail.com.

What is Forkcount?

Forkcount tracks how often you eat out (restaurants, delivery, takeout) and what it costs you, so you can eat out less and cook more. You log a meal in two taps, set a monthly cap, and watch your cooked-at-home streak grow. Everything works offline, with no account.

Where is my data stored? Do I need an account?

No account, ever. All your logs live in a local database on your phone. Nothing you log is sent to a server. Your history is included in your device's standard Android backup, so it survives a reinstall or a new phone. Details are in the privacy policy.

I forgot to log yesterday. Can I fix it?

Yes. If yesterday is empty, Today shows a "Nothing logged yesterday" card with one-tap options. For any older day, open History and tap the day — you can add an ate-out or cooked entry, edit a meal's type or cost, or delete a mistaken log.

How do streaks work? What's a streak freeze?

Your streak counts consecutive days without eating out. Everyone gets one streak freeze per month: a single missed or unlogged day won't break your streak — it shows as an outlined day on the calendar. Plus members can enable up to three freezes per month in Settings. A day you actually ate out always counts; freezes never cover those.

Why are older days in History greyed out?

The free app shows your last 4 weeks of history. Older logs are never deleted — they're stored on your device and included in CSV exports — but viewing them in the calendar is part of Forkcount Plus.

What does Plus include?

Unlimited history, extra streak freezes, themes, and a second home-screen widget. It's a subscription managed entirely through Google Play — cancel any time from Settings → Manage subscription, or in the Play Store under Subscriptions.

Can I get my data out? Can I bring data in?

Both. Settings → Export to CSV saves your complete history (including anything beyond the free 4-week window) wherever you choose. Settings → Import from CSV reads the same format back, and skips entries that are already present, so importing twice never creates duplicates.

What does the evening check-in notification do?

If you haven't logged anything by your check-in time, Forkcount asks "Did you eat out today?" You can answer right from the notification — "Yes" opens the log sheet, "No, I cooked" logs a cooked day. You can change the time or turn it off in Settings.

Does the app convert currencies?

No. The currency setting only changes the symbol shown next to amounts. Costs are stored as the numbers you enter, so switching currency doesn't change any values.

Why doesn't the widget update right away?

Android batches widget updates to save battery, so a change can take a moment. Logging from the widget itself updates it immediately.
