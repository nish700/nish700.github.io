2. Extended Family Scan

Nature of Service

The Extended Family feature compares a baby's photo against up to six family members (parents, siblings, grandparents, uncles, aunts) and ranks them by facial similarity, with an optional feature-by-feature breakdown. Like all KinScan comparisons, results are probabilistic, provided for entertainment and informational purposes only, and are not evidence of biological relationship, parentage, or ancestry.

Credit Usage

  • Extended Family scan: 5–12 credits depending on how many family members are compared (1 base credit plus 2 credits per member, for 2 to 6 members).
  • Optional feature breakdown: 2 credits per compared member, with a minimum of 6 credits.
  • Credits are deducted only after a scan completes successfully, and only for members actually compared. Family members whose photos could not be processed are excluded and not charged.
  • Scans stopped by the photo-quality or age checks are not charged.

Photo Requirements

  • Photos tagged as Mom, Dad, Grandma, or Grandpa must show an adult; the app will ask you to change photos that appear to show a child.
  • Clear, well-lit, single-person photos are required for accurate results. Low-quality or multi-person photos are flagged; proceeding anyway may reduce accuracy.

User Responsibilities

  • You must have permission to use every photo you upload, including from each adult family member depicted.
  • Photos of babies and children may only be uploaded by, or with the permission of, the child's parent or legal guardian.
  • Results (including shared leaderboard cards) must not be used to make, support, or imply claims about biological relationships.

3. Baby Prediction Feature

Nature of Service

The Baby Prediction feature (where available) uses artificial intelligence to generate an artistic interpretation of what a future baby might look like based on photos of two adults. This feature is provided solely for entertainment purposes.

Important Disclaimers

  • Not a Scientific Prediction: Generated images are AI-created artistic interpretations, NOT scientific genetic predictions or medical forecasts.
  • No Accuracy Guarantee: Results do not and cannot represent what your actual child will look like.
  • Entertainment Only: This feature should not be used for any medical, legal, or decision-making purposes.
  • Age Requirement: Both uploaded photos must be of individuals 18 years or older.

Image Processing

  • Parent images are temporarily uploaded to secure Firebase Storage for processing
  • Images are sent to third-party AI service (MaxStudio AI) for generation
  • Images are automatically deleted after baby generation completes
  • Generated baby images are stored locally on your device
  • We do not retain copies of your parent or baby images on our servers

Credit Usage

  • First baby generation: 2 credits
  • Subsequent generations: 5 credits
  • Credits are only deducted upon successful generation
  • Failed generations will not be charged
