Privacy Policy — Shabd Reveal

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Shabd Reveal (“the app”, “we”) is a daily crossword and word-puzzle game made by an independent developer in India. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version is below.

Everything stays on your device. Shabd Reveal does not collect any personal data, has no user accounts, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking. Your gameplay never leaves your phone.

Data stored on your device only

The app stores the following locally on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Puzzle progress, completions, and solve times
  • Scores, XP, levels, stars, and badges
  • Daily streaks
  • Your onboarding preferences (for example, the “solver persona” shown on the home screen)
  • Cached puzzles and hints that ship inside the app

This data may be included in your device’s own Google backup, which is controlled entirely by your Android backup settings, encrypted by Google, and inaccessible to us. Uninstalling the app removes all of its local data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts and does not transmit your gameplay, any identifier, or any personal information off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

Advertising

No ads. This app is completely ad-free and includes no advertising SDKs.

Permissions

The app declares standard network permissions, but this version does not use them to send any personal data. All daily puzzles are bundled inside the app and work fully offline.

What we never collect or access

  • No account, name, email, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera
  • No advertising ID or precise device identifiers

Children

Shabd Reveal is a general-audience word game. It does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights (India’s DPDP Act, GDPR & others)

Because the app holds no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. If you have any question about your privacy, contact us at the email below and we will respond.

A note on optional future features

A later version of the app may add an optional “Create your own puzzle” feature that would send a topic you type to a puzzle-generation service to build a custom crossword. The current version does not include or enable that feature, and sends no such data. If it is ever enabled, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what is sent and why.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
