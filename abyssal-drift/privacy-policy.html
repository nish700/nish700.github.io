Abyssal Drift — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Abyssal Drift" (package com.abyssaldrift)

Abyssal Drift is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, or location, and stores your game progress only on your device. This policy explains the limited data that the app's third-party services process, and your choices.

Data the app itself stores

Your game progress (levels completed, in-game currency balances, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Data processed by third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, which may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), coarse device information (model, OS version, language), and interaction events:

  • Google AdMob — shows optional rewarded advertisements and, where enabled, interstitial advertisements. AdMob may use identifiers for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. See Google's advertising policy.
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous gameplay events (for example, "level completed") used to improve difficulty and features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and device diagnostics used to fix bugs.
  • Google Play Billing — processes optional in-app purchases. We never see your payment details; Google Play handles the transaction and shares only an order confirmation with the app.

Consent

In regions where consent is required (including the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before advertising or analytics identifiers are used, using Google's consent framework. You can change your choice at any time from the in-game settings under "Privacy options." Analytics and crash reporting remain off until consent is resolved.

Children

Abyssal Drift is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad content is restricted to Google's "G" (general audiences) rating.

Your rights

Depending on your region (GDPR in the EEA/UK, DPDP Act in India, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete data, or to withdraw consent. Because the app holds no account data, most data is either on your device (delete by uninstalling) or held by Google under their policies. You can:

  • reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Android settings;
  • use in-game "Privacy options" to withdraw consent;
  • contact us for anything else at the address below.

Data retention and security

We retain no data ourselves. Data held by Google services is retained under their published retention policies (Firebase Analytics: up to 14 months). All network communication uses TLS encryption.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated and the new policy published at this address before the change takes effect.
